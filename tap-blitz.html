Tap Blitz

A square appears. You tap it. It gets hard quickly.

Get it on Google Play

Free for Android. Supported by ads, no purchases.

Three modes

Classic

The square shrinks the moment it appears. Tap it before it vanishes and the next one comes smaller and faster. One miss ends the run.

Time

Sixty seconds to tap as many squares as you can. The square waits for you, so it is pure speed. Tapping empty space costs 2 seconds.

100

Tap 100 squares as fast as you can. The clock counts up, a miss adds 2 seconds, and your best time is saved.

Simple and quick

  • Short runs that fit into a spare minute.
  • Your best result in every mode is saved on your device.
  • Optional haptics and sound, each with its own switch in Settings.
  • No account or sign-in, and it plays without an internet connection.
